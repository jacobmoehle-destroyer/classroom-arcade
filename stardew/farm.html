Pocket Farm

Grow a tiny garden, harvest crops, and expand your patch.

📅 Day 1
🪙 Coins 12
🌰 Seeds 5
🥕 Harvest 0
Choose Plant, then tap an empty patch. Water crops each day to help them grow.

Tip: plant → water → end day. Crops need water to grow. When ripe, select Harvest and tap them. This is an original mini-game; it is not Stardew Valley and uses no third-party game files.
