🧱 Classroom Sandbox

World controls and settings

Game mode

Creative has unlimited blocks. Survival adds health, hunger, resource collecting, food, and basic crafting.

🎒 Inventory & Crafting

Inventory loads here.

In Survival, broken blocks go into your inventory. Use the dedicated 🧰 Craft button to make tools, a crafting table, a bed, torches, planks, and sticks.

Graphics

Performance update: instanced chunk rendering reduces separate draw calls, nearby-only block raycasts, lower default pixel ratio, small chunk-generation batches, configurable render distance, and FPS display. The game preloads 30 nearby chunks before spawning. Use Battery Saver and Near render distance on older iPads/Chromebooks. Saves stay in this browser on this device. Day/night, new biomes, ores, armor, and crafting are included.

🎒 Inventory

Tap a block to equip it in your hotbar. In Survival, collected materials are limited and placing blocks uses them.

📦 Chest

Store items here for this world.

🧰 Crafting & Tools

Craft tools to mine tougher blocks. Crafted blocks and tools are saved with your inventory.

🪵 Materials & building

⛏️ Tools

Survival mining: stone, coal, and amethyst need a wooden pickaxe or better. Iron, copper, gold, emerald, diamond, and ancient debris need a stone pickaxe. New biomes include jungle, cherry grove, and mushroom regions. Armor reduces fall damage; day and night cycle automatically. Bedrock cannot be mined.
