Before adding a game

  • Confirm the game is suitable for your students and allowed by school policy.
  • Check its license; don't upload copyrighted game files or ROMs without permission.
  • Test in Safari on the actual iPad, including touch controls, audio, landscape mode, and any sign-in prompts.
  • Ask IT to approve the specific game domain. Hosting this page on GitHub Pages does not unblock other sites.
  • Prefer games with no account requirement, no chat, minimal tracking, and no external purchases.
